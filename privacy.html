Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Upload Helper is used only by its owner to upload the owner's own videos as drafts to the owner's own TikTok accounts.

Data we store

The tool stores the TikTok access token and refresh token of the connected accounts, the account display name, and the open ID. They are stored in the owner's private Google Apps Script project and used only to upload drafts and check their upload status.

Data we do not collect

The tool does not collect data from other TikTok users, does not track visitors, and uses no cookies or analytics.

Sharing

No data is sold or shared with third parties.

Deletion

Access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app under Settings → Security → Apps and services. The owner can delete the stored tokens from the Apps Script project at any time.
